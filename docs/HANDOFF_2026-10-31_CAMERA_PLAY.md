## 5. Methodology and decision record

Execution order of the reader (`src/features/camera/useCamera.ts`, `process` → `resolve`):

1. **Frame:** draw the video to a canvas (≤ 640 px wide). Grid work uses a separate 360-px-wide copy (`gridView`); detection is unreliable at other sizes.
2. **Grid:** `autoGrid` (`vision.ts`) — wood-colour blob → rough quad → coarse shift/scale search → best 3 candidates each refined (projection-profile snap + corner polish) → accept if `gridLocked` (≤ 3 weak lines of 20) **or** `fitsGame` (the occupancy read through the grid matches any position of the game within 2 squares, either 180° orientation). On panel start, a saved grid that does not `fitsGame` triggers a full re-search.
3. **Occupancy:** per-square texture, min-max normalised, hysteresis (empty < 0.24, occupied > 0.32, cut 0.26), red ink counts as occupied; the prior frame's reading is the hysteresis prior.
4. **Stability:** a motion gate, then identical occupancy for 2 frames.
5. **Stateless resolve:** compare the whole-board occupancy with the game record every still frame:
   - equals the current position → in sync (anchors a reference frame for capture inference);
   - a held AI reply exists (vs AI, camera live) → the board equals current+reply (or current+reply+your move, or your move with the reply skipped) → `land()` plays the AI move; if the board only differs on the reply's squares → ask to play it (arrow shown on the app board);
   - equals an earlier position → if the opponent is to move there, ask for that move; otherwise take back (spar `takeBack`, analyze `setCursor(-1)`), repeated until it matches;
   - current + one legal move of yours → classify (ONNX piece model for drops/promotions, appearance diff for captures) and commit;
   - with AI moves not yet reproduced on the board, a legal move of yours that explains every square outside those AI moves → commit it (reminder stays up);
   - otherwise → step instructions (move A→B, place, remove), after ~1 s; "something over the board" only after ~3 s.
6. **Phone link (`phone.ts`):** the computer's panel always listens on its own desk ID and on `shogigym-room-<sha256(public IPv4)[0..8]>` (public address from a STUN `srflx` candidate, 10 s timeout, 3 attempts). The phone page calls its saved desk ID and the room in parallel and keeps the first that connects; a 6 s watchdog rebuilds the call while disconnected. The sender asks for 1080p, sets `contentHint='detail'`, `degradationPreference='maintain-resolution'`, `maxBitrate` 6 Mbit/s.
7. **AI hold:** `useOpponent(…, { hold })` does not auto-play the reply while the camera panel is open and streaming; `land()` plays it. The board shows the reply as the existing blue `REPLY_BLUE` arrow; the header says 「AIの手 … を盤で指してください」.

Decisions to preserve:

- **Stateless over baseline tracking:** baseline tracking drifted (camera at ply 42 while the app was at 44) and could never recover. Do not reintroduce a camera-side "physical position".
- **No periodic grid re-snap:** small snaps accumulated into drift. The grid changes only on tap, the re-detect button, or a board-moved state lasting about 3 s.
- **`fitsGame` acceptance:** semantic check over a looser line rule, because faint/compressed lines fail line rules while a shifted grid fails the game check.
- **PeerJS public broker for signalling only:** no own server; video is peer-to-peer.
- **Eval graph:** the rated move's after-score is from the mover's side (`useAutoRate.ts`); a mate-0 score means the side to move is mated (`engine.ts` `scoreToCp`).
