### 5.2 Legal frame used

- Japanese copyright law has no general fair use and no non-commercial exemption for publication; private-use (Art. 30) does not cover a public app; quotation (Art. 32) requires the quoted part to be subordinate and clearly marked — lessons built on another's article fail that.
- Not protected: facts, ideas, rules, castle shapes, strategy names/plans, 棋譜 (move sequences of games and opening theory).
- Protected: explanatory text, captions, headings, annotations (annotated 棋譜), composed tsume and constructed teaching positions, creative selections/arrangements (collections) — and translations/adaptations of these (翻訳権・翻案権).
- Therefore: delete rather than "re-paraphrase"; write new material with own structure, own positions, own words; adapt only CC BY-SA text with attribution.

### 5.5 Mechanical gates (why these thresholds)

- `scripts/lessons/originality.ts`: normalize (NFKC; strip move notation like ▲７六歩/△同銀; keep only kana/kanji/ー); 10-gram index over `.cache/curriculum-source` + `.cache/reference`; per string: longest shared runs and coverage. Fail when any run ≥ 16 chars or coverage > 25% (strings ≥ 40 chars). At 14 chars generic phrases ("マスずつ動けるようになります") false-flagged; at 16/25% the detector still flags 2018 of 2242 old "paraphrases".
- Position check (`check-unit`): any node whose board+hands+turn equals a figure in `.cache/old` is rejected unless the chapter is an opening from startpos/handicap and the position has full 40-piece material.
- `kind: opening` chapters must start from `startpos` or one of `HANDICAPS` in `unit.ts`.
- Adapted references must carry a free license (`FREE_LICENSES` in `unit.ts`).
- Opening lines: Peta compact book lookup (`scripts/lessons/book.ts`, uses `src/utils/bookPosition.ts` for gote rotation and hand ordering) → NOTE for moves outside the book; `--engine` → NOTE for moves losing > 300 cp.

### 5.6 Lesson writing method (the bar every unit must meet)

1. Study: read `.cache/curriculum-source/<study-id>.txt` completely and `.cache/old/lessons/figures/<study-id>.json` (positions, stated lines) to understand the ideas, plans, reasons and typical mistakes.
2. Close the source. Design an own chapter structure and order; own titles; own sentences. Never mirror headings, figure order or captions; never translate/paraphrase sentences.
3. Positions: openings from startpos (facts, verified with book + engine); everything else composed new (rules, tesuji, castles as positions, attacks, endgame) and engine-verified; mates confirmed by engine mate distance or taken from `src/data/tsume.json`.
4. Japanese first (natural, accurate, beginner-friendly), English = translation of own Japanese. Square names in Japanese notation so the board highlights them.
5. 3–6 chapters, 3–10 steps each, marks where useful, variations for key alternatives.
6. References: Wikipedia pages used (license, adapted flag); the studied article as `{"license": "All rights reserved", "adapted": false}` (consulted only).
7. `bun scripts/lessons/check-unit.ts <id> [--engine]` until 0 ERROR.
8. Orchestrator review: read every step's Japanese text against the board; verify every factual rule claim; fix or remove anything uncertain (example: the 500手 持将棋 claim was removed).
