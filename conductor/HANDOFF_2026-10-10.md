## 5. Methodology and decision record

1. **Legal frame.** Japanese law: no fair use, no non-commercial publication exemption; facts/ideas and 棋譜 free; text, annotations, composed positions (tsume, teaching diagrams) and creative selections/collections protected; translations and close paraphrases are derivatives. Therefore: remove rather than "paraphrase better"; write new material with own structure.
2. **Removal over rewrite where the content is the source's expression.** Old lessons (captions, explanations, structure, translations) and shogi-rule tesuji diagrams/kif collection were deleted, not reworded.
3. **Kept:** vendored Shiryu joseki (GPL; lines are opening theory; flagged notes rewritten), Wikipedia-derived courses (CC BY-SA, attributed), single game records (millennium = 将棋倶楽部24 game; hidarimino = 1996 pro game), YaneuraOu tsume (computer-generated), Peta book (MIT).
4. **Mechanical gates** (`docs/lessons.md`): Japanese text normalized (move notation stripped), 10-gram index over the corpus; fail on any shared run ≥16 chars or >25% coverage; reduced-material positions equal to any old figure rejected; `kind: opening` only from startpos/handicap starts; adapted references must be free licenses.
5. **User-provided links:** mafu-opening-theory (GPL-3.0, usable for lines), shogidb2/joseki (no license → reference only), lishogi studies and Reddit (authors keep rights → reference only).
6. **Scope correction:** a 146-unit plan with ~90 opening lessons duplicated the joseki courses and was run as a parallel fleet. Rule now in `AGENTS.md`: openings live in courses; units written one at a time, at most one writer.
