## 5. Methodology and decision record

- **Voice:** clips are pre-recorded offline, not synthesized in the browser.
  - The in-browser VOICEVOX WASM build takes about 5 s per phrase, and the owner chose "record and convert".
  - WebM/Opus was used at the owner's request.
  - Katakana input is used because the engine misread たたきのふ as たたきのお.
  - Credit 「VOICEVOX:ずんだもん」 is required by zunko.jp/con_ongen_kiyaku.html; it appears in Settings and the README.
- **Eval bar:** a full-height strip at the left edge of the board area (`src/workshop/stage/EvalBar.tsx`, inside `.ws-board-wrap.with-eval`).
  - This placement was the owner's explicit chess.com reference.
  - It is held steady by `useSteadyRate` so it does not twitch.
- **Reel:** rendered on a virtual clock rather than a screen recording. Recording ran at 10 fps and the owner asked for 60.
  - The camera stays on the board's sides and top. The owner rejected shots that pass behind the players' heads.
- **SEO:** canonical URLs end in `/` because GitHub Pages 301-redirects `/play` to `/play/`.
